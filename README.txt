ECOMHUB WHOLESALE CATALOGUE

Open index.html in a modern browser. The catalogue is static and works without
installing packages or connecting to a server. Keep the assets folder, data.js,
app.js, and styles.css beside index.html.

The site includes products and catalogue prices transcribed from the supplied
PDF. Its product photos have been converted to small WebP images for faster
loading. The catalogue price note is shown in the site because the source PDF
says prices may differ when placing an order.

Features: product search, category filters, price sorting, a saved enquiry list,
and WhatsApp enquiry sharing. "Share enquiry list" opens WhatsApp for the
user-provided number 8249918763 with product names, quantities, unit prices,
line totals, the estimated catalogue total, and the price note already filled
in. The user reviews the message and taps Send in WhatsApp.
